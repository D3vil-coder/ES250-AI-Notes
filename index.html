Weather vs Climate — 30-year rule
  • Weather: short-term, local — temp, humidity, wind right now.
  • Climate: 30-year statistical average (WMO). May snowstorm = weather; decades of warmer oceans = climate.
  • CO₂: ice cores ~350 ppm [per transcript] → Mauna Loa >415 ppm today.
GWP₁₀₀: CO₂ = 1 · CH₄ = 23 · N₂O = 296
→ 1 ton CH₄ ≈ 23 tons CO₂ of heat trapping
Why methane traps more? Molecular bonds vibrate at same frequencies as outgoing infrared. CH₄ geometry absorbs those wavelengths far better than CO₂ — a thicker blanket per ton.
Forcing vs feedback: Forcing = energy imbalance (in shortwave, out longwave, GHGs send IR back). Positive feedback amplifies: ice-albedo melt → dark ocean absorbs → more melt; wildfires → CO₂ → more warming. Negative feedback resists: oceans absorb ~¼ excess CO₂ — but form carbonic acid → ocean acidification (pH ↓, shells dissolve).

Gibbs diagram (1970) — the boomerang →
  • Bottom-right — precipitation dominance: just gathered rain, low TDS, Na-rich.

Buffers & scale · 36:40 – 41:16

Alkalinity is a shock absorber. Hardness is a nuisance.

Related, not same. Plus what you can see and feel: TDS, turbidity, colour, temperature.

Alkalinity = acid-neutralizing capacity (HCO₃⁻, CO₃²⁻, OH⁻). Hardness = multivalent cations >+1, overwhelmingly Ca²⁺ + Mg²⁺ → soap scum + boiler scale.

  • Permanent (non-carbonate): Ca/Mg + Cl⁻/SO₄²⁻ → boiling does nothing → lime-soda or ion-exchange resin.
